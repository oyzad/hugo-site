What is meaning

One of the greatest questions one can ask themselves is ‘What is my meaning’. From evolution’s perspective everything exists to reproduce, however humans are self aware so are able to find their meaning for themselves. I believe that meaning comes from engaging with the world around you: challenging yourself, exploring the world, contributing to something bigger than ourselves. Humanity considering its flaws is able to grow and adapt, so being human is the ability to be curious and open to change.
